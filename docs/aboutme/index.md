#About Me

Pierce Brown

I am a curious, hands-on problem solver who has always been interested in understanding how things work. From an early age, I enjoyed taking apart robotic toys and electronics to see what was inside and how they worked. As I have grown older, that curiosity has continued through practical tasks such as working on cars, equipment, and other mechanical challenges. 

These experiences are what led me towards mechanical engineering and the University of North Carolina at Charlotte. I enjoy working with both my hands and my mind, especially when a problem requires figuring out how things work and fixing or improving them. Mechanical engineering gives me a way to build on and focus the curiosity I have had throughout my life while developing the technical knowledge and problem-solving skills needed to approach complex problems professionally.

As I work towards becoming a mechanical engineer, I want to become someone who can combine practical experience with sound engineering principles. I am interested not only in finding a solution, but also in understanding why that solution works and whether it is the best choice for the problem at hand. I am developing the ability to analyze problems, apply theory, test ideas, and communicate my reasoning clearly. My goal is to become an engineer who is capable of taking an unfamiliar problem, breaking it down into manageable parts, and developing a solution that is supported by evidence rather than assumptions.
